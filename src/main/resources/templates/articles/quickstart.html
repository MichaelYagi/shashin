Quick Start

  1. For first time website setup, register your username and password on the registration page.
  2. Login as an administrator with the username and password you registered with.
    1. Subsequent registrations need to processed by an administrator on the User Management page.
  4. Once logged in as an administrator, go to the settings page from the sidebar.
  5. In the "General" tab in the "Media directories" text area, point to where your photos or videos are stored, separating multiple directories with a comma.
    1. Eg. /Volumes/Pictures, /Volumes/Home Movies
  6. Click "Save" near the bottom of the page.
  7. In the "Media Indexing" tab, click the "Scan" button.
  8. After you see the message "Scan Complete", view your media on the timeline page.

Tips & Tricks

Filters

For the timeline, explore (excluding folders), favorites and album pages, you can filter by media type or media with no coordinate data. In albums, you can also filter by media with comments.

For example,

  • /timeline/video filters media by video
  • /taken/gif filters media by gifs
  • /recent/nolatlng filters media that are missing coordinates
  • /album/1/comments filters media that have comments

Pagination

You can view recently added, modified, taken or accessed pages with pagination. Access by specifying the page index (starting from 0) and the media type.

You can jump to pages using the pagination navigation at the bottom of the page.

For example,

  • /recent/0/all goes to the first page and does not filter results
  • /taken/0/gif filters media by gifs and view the first page
  • /recent/1/nolatlng filters media that are missing coordinates and view the second page
  • /album/1/2/comments filters media that have comments and view the third page

Multi Select

You can multi select photos, which is useful if editing multiple photos.

  • Start by selecting a photo in multi select mode in the upper left corner of a photo
  • Hover over the last photo you want to select in a range of photos and press the shift to select the entire range of photos. The first photo in the range should have a blue outline.
    • Mobile: double tap the last photo you want to select in a range of photos.
  • You can deselect the photos by deselecting a photo within the range and press the shift to deselect the range of photos.
    • Mobile: double tap to deselect the range of photos.
